I am a Computer Science PhD student at the University of Nebraska–Lincoln, working with Prof. Liang He in the Embedded Sensing and IoT group. My research focuses on energy harvesting and self-powered sensing systems for vehicular and mobile IoT applications.
Before joining UNL, I received my M.S. in Smart City Engineering from Hanyang University, where I worked on drone battery management under the supervision of Prof. [Youngmoon Lee](https://sites.google.com/umich.edu/youngmoonlee/home). I also earned my B.S. in Robotics Engineering from Hanyang University.
